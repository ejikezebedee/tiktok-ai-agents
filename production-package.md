# TIKTOK PRODUCTION PACKAGE — "AI AGENTS: From Answers to Action"

## 1. Final Video Title
**"AI Agents Don't Just Answer — They Work For You"**

## 2. Alternative Hooks (pick one)
- **Hook A:** "ChatGPT answering your questions is only the beginning."
- **Hook B:** "AI that answers questions is old news. AI that does the work? That's the shift."
- **Hook C:** "Stop asking AI questions. Start giving it goals."

## 3. Final Narration Script (~145 words / ~42s)

[0–3s] ChatGPT answering your questions is only the beginning.

[3–10s] Until now, AI mostly answered. You ask, it replies. That's a chatbot.

[10–20s] Now meet the AI agent. You don't ask it questions — you give it a goal. It plans the steps, chooses its tools, and does the work.

[20–32s] Here's a real example. A business owner says: "Find me potential customers and build a report." The agent researches companies, gathers public data, organizes it, analyzes the opportunities, and writes the report. Then it pauses — and asks for your approval.

[32–40s] Soon, businesses will run on teams of humans plus specialized agents. One researches. One sells. One codes. One handles data. One runs operations. You supervise.

[40–45s] The future isn't humans versus AI. It's people who know how to use AI agents — versus those who don't. Follow for practical AI-agent videos.
