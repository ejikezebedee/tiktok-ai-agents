## 3. Final Narration (~130 words / ~50s)

[0–2s] You don't need a big team to build a business anymore.

[2–8s] An AI agent can research your market and validate your idea before you spend a kobo.

[8–14s] It can build your brand — the name, logo ideas, and your first message.

[14–20s] It can handle customers — answering questions and drafting replies you approve.

[20–26s] It can help you sell — finding leads, writing follow-ups, and preparing proposals.

[26–32s] It can run the back office — invoices, expenses, and a weekly report.

[32–38s] It can grow your content — turning one idea into posts and scripts.

[38–44s] And it can coordinate all of it while you stay in charge.

[44–50s] Start with one process. Automate it. Then add the next. Follow for practical AI growth.
