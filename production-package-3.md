# TIKTOK PRODUCTION PACKAGE — "Practical AI Examples You Can Use Today"

## 1. Final Video Title
**"Forget the AI Hype — Here's What AI Can Actually Do For You"**

## 2. Alternative Hooks (pick one)
- **Hook A:** "Forget the AI hype. Here's what AI can actually do for you today."
- **Hook B:** "You don't need to be a programmer to make AI useful."
- **Hook C:** "AI isn't a toy. Here's the work it can take off your plate today."

## 3. Final Narration (~139 words / ~55s)

[0–2s] Forget the AI hype. Here's what AI can do for you today.

[2–8s] Give AI messy notes, and it turns them into a summary, action list, email, or plan.

[8–14s] Instead of reading a fifty-page document, AI summarizes it and answers your questions.

[14–20s] AI turns a rough idea into a professional email, proposal, or reply.

[20–26s] Upload your sales data, and AI finds patterns and explains what's happening.

[26–32s] AI researches markets, compares competitors, and organizes it into a report.

[32–37s] AI understands images too — identify objects, or explain a screenshot.

[37–42s] Use AI as a tutor, explaining anything at your level.

[42–48s] And AI connects to your tools to do real work — reading messages, updating your CRM — with you approving.

[48–55s] AI's real value isn't random questions. It's removing work from your day. Start with one task you repeat weekly. Follow for practical AI.
