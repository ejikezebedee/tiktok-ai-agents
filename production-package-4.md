[0–2s] You're still doing work an AI agent could do for you.

[2–8s] It can read your inbox, draft the replies, and flag what actually needs you.

[8–14s] It can turn a messy voice note into a clean list of tasks and decisions.

[14–20s] It can find local customers and build you a clean lead list.

[20–26s] It can chase unpaid invoices and flag the ones that are overdue.

[26–32s] It can turn a rough idea into a professional proposal or quote.

[32–38s] It can turn your sales numbers into a simple weekly summary.

[38–44s] And it can reuse one post across your email, social, and scripts.

[44–50s] Stop doing the busy work. Let the agent handle it — while you approve the important parts. Follow for practical AI you can actually use.
