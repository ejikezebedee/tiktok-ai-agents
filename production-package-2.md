# TIKTOK PRODUCTION PACKAGE — "5 Things an AI Agent Can Already Do for Your Business"

## 1. Final Video Title
**"5 Things an AI Agent Can Already Do for Your Business"**

## 2. Alternative Hooks (pick one)
- **Hook A:** "If you're only using AI to ask questions, you're barely using AI."
- **Hook B:** "AI that answers is last year. AI that does the work is now."
- **Hook C:** "Stop asking AI questions. Start giving it work to do."

## 3. Final Narration Script (~121 words / ~48s)

[0–4s] If you're only using AI to ask questions, you're barely using AI. An AI agent can actually do the work.

[4–12s] It can read your incoming emails, spot what matters, draft replies, and flag anything that needs you.

[12–19s] It can research potential customers, organize public info, and build a lead report.

[19–26s] It can check your calendar, find open times, and prepare appointments.

[26–33s] Give it your business data, and it can analyze it and build a clean report.

[33–41s] And one agent can coordinate several tools, or even other agents, while you supervise.

[41–49s] The biggest change with AI isn't better answers. It's AI that takes action. Businesses that learn AI agents early get a serious advantage. Follow for practical AI-agent examples.
